## ⚠️ Note: Fiverr/Upwork at 17

Both platforms require 18+ ID verification. The legit workaround used by many
young freelancers with parent support:

1. **Your dad creates the account** under his name + verified ID
2. He adds you as the person doing the work (allowed — it's his business account, you're the operator; be honest in the profile if asked)
3. Payouts go to his PayPal/Payoneer → his GCash/bank
4. You do the work, reply to clients, deliver gigs

Start with FB/direct clients FIRST (no ID needed, faster money), then expand to
Fiverr once you have 2–3 demos + reviews from local clients to show off. Local
proof = faster Fiverr sales.
