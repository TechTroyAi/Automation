# Outreach scripts — start a few relevant conversations

The portfolio is evidence, not a traffic source by itself. Personalize each message,
contact only relevant business channels, follow group rules, and honor opt-outs.
No reply or revenue target is guaranteed. Use the public demo link only after the
reviewed PR is merged and deployed. Review contact/payment arrangements with your
guardian first; see [LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md](LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md).

## 1. First message to a shop (English)

> Hi [Shop Name]! I'm Troy. I build FAQ chatbots for small businesses.
> I noticed [one specific, true observation about their public Page].
>
> If answering repeated menu/hours/delivery questions takes your time, I can
> help set up approved replies for your Page. Here's a fictional shop demo
> you can try: https://techtroyai.github.io/Automation/#demo
>
> Would a short example using your public FAQs be useful? No obligation.

Do not pretend you have audited their inbox, know they are losing sales, or have
already worked with similar clients if that is not true.

## 2. First message (Bisaya)

## 3. Pilot invitation (first 1–2 clients only)

> I'm looking for one or two businesses for a small free or discounted pilot,
> subject to fit and availability. We could start with [one specific FAQ flow].
>
> We'll agree the scope, cost (including any tools), timeline, and support limit
> before starting. After you try it, I'd appreciate honest feedback—not a required
> positive review—and permission to show a redacted demo. I'll ask separately
> before publishing your words, business name, or logo and disclose the pilot.
>
> Would you like to discuss whether it fits your shop?

Confirm the price in writing; this is not an unlimited free-work promise.
If permission isn't granted, don't publish their identity or private data.

## 4. One follow-up (after 2–3 days, unless they opted out)

> Hi [Shop Name], just following up once on the FAQ-bot example. If it's useful,
> here's the demo again: https://techtroyai.github.io/Automation/#demo
> If it's not a priority, no worries—I won't keep messaging.

No response after that → stop. No mass automated DMs, pressure, or fake urgency.

## 5. When they ask “How much?” / “Pila?”

> Starter is from ₱1,500 for a simple FAQ bot, setup, and a short guide.
> Business is from ₱3,000 for the bot plus one simple automation.
> Standalone automation starts at ₱2,000; editing starts at ₱300 per video,
> or the listed ₱1,000 bundle for five short videos.
>
> I'll confirm a fixed scope and quote, including any separate tool costs.
> Standard terms are 50% after scope approval and 50% after you approve the
> working demo. If we're doing a pilot, we'll agree those terms separately.
>
> What task would you most like to simplify?

## 6. Interested client → written scope

> Great! Please send your public menu/service list and the questions customers
> ask most often. No passwords, OTPs, or private customer records needed.
>
> I'll propose the deliverable, price, tool costs, timeline, revision limit,
> acceptance checklist, and support period. Once we've agreed, we'll confirm
> the payment recipient privately through this verified contact channel.
> Account access should use official invitations with only necessary permissions.

## 7. Common objections

**“Can you guarantee more sales?”**
> No. The goal is more consistent replies and less repetitive work. We'll test
> the agreed questions together; I won't promise revenue or perfect AI answers.

**“Do you have client reviews?”**
> Not published yet. You can test my self-built demo and inspect the source.
> I'm looking for a small pilot to earn real feedback, not invent testimonials.

**“Can you build it for free?”**
> I may be able to offer one of the first two small pilot builds, depending on
> fit and availability. Let's define one useful task and agree the limits first.

**“I need to think.”**
> Of course. Here's the demo for whenever you're ready. No pressure.

## 8. Video editing inquiry

> Hi [Shop Name]! I'm Troy, and I edit short Reels/TikToks with captions and hooks.
> I noticed [specific public video and a respectful, practical suggestion].
> Would you like to discuss a short sample using footage you own? We can agree
> the sample scope and cost before starting; music and footage permissions matter.

Only attach a direct, working sample that you have permission to show.
