## Script 1: Cold DM to Local Shops (English — FB Page message)

Send to: restaurants, milk tea shops, boutiques, resellers, salons in Tubod / LDN.
Find them: search "Tubod LDN [shop type]" on Facebook → open their Page → Message.

> Hi [Shop Name]! 👋 I'm Troy, a 17-year-old from Tubod, LDN who builds AI auto-reply
> chatbots for Facebook Pages.
>
> I noticed your page — when customers message asking for prices/hours/location
> and you're busy, some of them probably don't wait and just buy somewhere else.
> Sayang 😅
>
> I can set up an AI that replies INSTANTLY to those common questions, 24/7 —
> even at midnight, even when you're busy. One-time setup, only ₱1,500, and you
> approve a working demo before paying the balance.
>
> Here's what I've built: [YOUR PORTFOLIO LINK]
>
> Want me to show you a 1-minute demo of how it would work for [Shop Name]?
> Free, no commitment 🙏

**Why this works:** specific problem (missed messages) → specific fix → price upfront
→ proof link → tiny ask (watch a demo, not "hire me"). Low pressure = more replies.

## Script 3: Follow-up (send after 2–3 days of no reply)

> Hi [Shop Name]! Just following up on my message about the auto-reply chatbot 🙂
> No pressure at all — but if you're losing even 2–3 customers a month to slow
> replies, the bot pays for itself.
>
> Want the free 1-min demo? I can make one specific for your shop today.
> Here's my work again: [YOUR PORTFOLIO LINK]

**Rule:** follow up ONCE. If still no reply, move on — there are thousands of shops.
Never double-message angrily. Your reputation > one client.

> And the setup protects you: you approve a working demo BEFORE paying the balance.
> Zero risk on your side. Plus payments go through my dad's verified GCash, fully traceable.

**"₱1,500 is expensive."**

> then it's free profit forever. It's a one-time payment, not monthly. And honestly,

**"I need to think about it."**
> Of course, take your time! 👍 I'll be here. Just so you know, I only take [2–3]
> projects at a time so I can focus — message me when ready and I'll confirm my
> slot. Here's my portfolio again for when you decide: [LINK]

> of real work. What I CAN do: the demo is free to watch, and you only pay the
> balance after approving it. That's the fairest deal I can give 🙏
> (Never work for "exposure." Exposure doesn't load GCash. 😤)

> I saw your videos — your products are nice, pero mas mopalit ang tao kung naay
> captions + hook sa first 2 seconds. Most people scroll with sound OFF, so no
> captions = no sales 😅
>
> I can edit 5 videos for ₱1,000 (captions + hooks + trending sounds included),
> 48-hour turnaround. Want me to edit ONE short clip as a FREE sample so you can
> see the difference? 🎬
>
> My work: [YOUR PORTFOLIO LINK]

**The free sample trick:** editing ONE 15-sec clip takes you 20 mins but converts
like crazy. Only offer it to shops that post often (repeat customers = recurring income).
